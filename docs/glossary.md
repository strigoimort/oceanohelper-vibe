# Glossary

This glossary defines the common scientific and technical terms used throughout OceanoHelper.

---

## General

**GIS**
Geographic Information System.

**CRS**
Coordinate Reference System.

**WGS84**
World Geodetic System 1984 (EPSG:4326), the default coordinate system used by OceanoHelper.

**UTC**
Coordinated Universal Time.

---

## Oceanography

**SST**
Sea Surface Temperature.

**SSTA**
Sea Surface Temperature Anomaly.

**SLA**
Sea Level Anomaly.

**SLP**
Sea Level Pressure.

**MSL**
Mean Sea Level.

**Chlorophyll-a**
An indicator of phytoplankton concentration in seawater.

---

## Waves

**Hs**
Significant Wave Height. The average height of the highest one-third of observed waves.

**Hmax**
Maximum Wave Height recorded during an observation period.

**Hmean**
Mean Wave Height.

**Tp**
Peak Wave Period.

**Tm**
Mean Wave Period.

**Tz**
Zero Crossing Period.

**Wave Rose**
A circular diagram showing the frequency distribution of wave direction and wave height.

---

## Wind

**Wind Rose**
A circular diagram showing the frequency distribution of wind direction and wind speed.

**Beaufort Scale**
An internationally recognized scale used to classify wind speed.

---

## Tides

**Formzahl Number (F)**
A ratio used to classify tidal type.

**Diurnal Tide**
One high tide and one low tide per day.

**Semidiurnal Tide**
Two high tides and two low tides of similar height per day.

**Mixed Tide**
A combination of diurnal and semidiurnal characteristics.

**MHW**
Mean High Water.

**MLW**
Mean Low Water.

**MHHW**
Mean Higher High Water.

**MLLW**
Mean Lower Low Water.

**LAT**
Lowest Astronomical Tide.

**HAT**
Highest Astronomical Tide.

---

## Harmonic Constituents

**M2**
Principal lunar semidiurnal constituent.

**S2**
Principal solar semidiurnal constituent.

**N2**
Larger lunar elliptic semidiurnal constituent.

**K1**
Luni-solar diurnal constituent.

**O1**
Principal lunar diurnal constituent.

**P1**
Principal solar diurnal constituent.

**K2**
Luni-solar semidiurnal constituent.

**Q1**
Larger lunar elliptic diurnal constituent.

---

## Climate

**ENSO**
El Niño–Southern Oscillation.

**ONI**
Oceanic Niño Index.

**IOD**
Indian Ocean Dipole.

**DMI**
Dipole Mode Index.

**El Niño**
A warm phase of ENSO characterized by above-average sea surface temperatures in the central and eastern equatorial Pacific Ocean.

**La Niña**
A cool phase of ENSO characterized by below-average sea surface temperatures in the central and eastern equatorial Pacific Ocean.

**Positive IOD**
A climate condition where the western Indian Ocean is warmer than the eastern Indian Ocean.

**Negative IOD**
A climate condition where the eastern Indian Ocean is warmer than the western Indian Ocean.

---

## Geospatial

**Point**
A single geographic location defined by latitude and longitude.

**Polyline**
A connected series of line segments.

**Polygon**
A closed shape representing an area.

**Rectangle**
A rectangular polygon.

**Circle**
A geometry defined by a center point and radius.

**Layer**
A collection of related geographic features displayed on the map.

**Basemap**
The underlying map used as a reference for spatial visualization.

---

## Particle Tracking

**Trajectory**
The path followed by a moving object over time.

**Historical Trajectory**
The observed movement of a particle based on recorded data.

**Forecast Trajectory**
The predicted future movement of a particle based on selected models or environmental conditions.

---

## Raster

**Raster**
A grid-based dataset representing spatial variables.

**GeoTIFF**
A georeferenced raster image format.

**NetCDF**
A scientific data format commonly used for atmospheric and oceanographic datasets.

**Interpolation**
A method used to estimate values between observed data points.

**IDW**
Inverse Distance Weighting interpolation.

**Kriging**
A geostatistical interpolation method.

**Nearest Neighbor**
An interpolation method that assigns the value of the closest observation.

**WMS**
Web Map Service.

**WMTS**
Web Map Tile Service.

**XYZ Tiles**
A tiled web mapping format commonly used by online basemaps.
